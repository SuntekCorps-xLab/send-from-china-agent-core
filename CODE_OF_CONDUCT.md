# Code of Conduct

We want project spaces to be useful, respectful, and safe for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be constructive, focus criticism on the work, respect privacy, and avoid
harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or publishing
another person's private information. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
limit participation, or ban participants whose behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Good-faith reporters and
participants must not face retaliation.
